1. Executive Summary

During operational threat monitoring across the enterprise messaging perimeter, the Splunk SIEM platform detected an orchestrated, multi-vector phishing campaign targeting corporate identities. By analyzing real-time email security gateway telemetry (index=email sourcetype="email:security"), the SOC isolated 8 high-risk malicious messages spanning credential harvesting, weaponized attachment delivery, executive impersonation (Whaling), and mass-blast phishing lures.

Automated gateway enforcement quarantined 5 messages and dropped 1 direct binary dropper at perimeter edge. Two messages were delivered into user inboxes prior to dynamic reputation scoring; however, immediate SOC playbook execution triggered administrative mailbox purging, credential revocation, and Sysmon endpoint telemetry verification, resulting in zero account compromises or endpoint malware executions.

6. Incident Remediation & Defense-in-Depth Recommendations

  1. Perimeter IOC Blocking: Added adversary relays (185.220.101.45, 194.26.29.112, 91.240.118.22) to firewall drop rules; added all extracted domains to SWG blocklists.
  2. Mailbox Eviction: Executed administrative search-and-purge to remove delivered messages matching the mass-blast HR policy and CEO whaling subjects.
  3. Strict DMARC Policy Enforcement: Transitioned corporate email domain DMARC policy from p=none to p=reject to eliminate inbound direct domain spoofing.
  4. Attachment Policy Hardening: Configured email gateway to immediately reject direct executables, dual-extension files, and disk container formats (.iso, .vhd, .img).
  5. Phishing-Resistant MFA: Confirmed mandatory FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware key authentication across all corporate accounts, rendering credential-harvesting attacks ineffective.
